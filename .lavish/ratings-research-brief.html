04 / Boundaries before rating approval

The MVP can explain these gaps.
It cannot score them away.

Shared ONyc requirements

  • Legal claim: signed participation terms, account rights and recovery priority.
  • Portfolio: dated reserve allocations, underwriting concentrations, claims and reserving.
  • Valuation: economic NAV, offer price and loss recognition reconciled.
  • Assurance: current attestations, custodians, authorities and deployed audit match.

Position and transformation requirements

  • Mandate: exact series, size, horizon, investor access and target cash asset.
  • Execution: settlement outputs, wrapper conversion and quotes for the intended size.
  • Seniority: current loss waterfall, oracle, recovery and withdrawal conditions.
  • Methodology: approved score formation, tranche treatment and independent review.
