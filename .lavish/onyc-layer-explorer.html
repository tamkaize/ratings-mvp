03 / How does the position become cash?

Follow the route, one step at a time

    Every arrow needs evidence

    For each step, identify the input and output asset, exact accounts, permissions, timing, fees, capacity, failure mode, and observed date.

    These are research routes, not verified swap instructions. The exact PT accounting unit and settlement output are unresolved.

    Why settlement is not the same as exit

    A PT can mature while a downstream withdrawal is unavailable. Returning a claim or wrapper token does not prove you can receive your chosen cash asset immediately.

    Primary ONyc redemption and a secondary sale are alternatives with different eligibility and liquidity requirements. Verify each rather than assuming either is always available.

    04 / Why an extra layer can change the loss

    Senior protection needs its own loss model

    Junior capital absorbs losses before senior capital. That can reduce a senior holder’s exposure to a covered loss, while the holder still depends on the same underlying and additional controls. Exponent’s tranche description.

    Teaching example: initial junior capital 20, senior capital 80. These are invented pool amounts, not observed srONyc coverage or Exponent’s coverage denominator. Assumes an immediate, enforceable waterfall; ignores fees, yield, pauses, and loss-recognition timing.

    The methodology gap

    Current propagation starts with the riskiest material child, then adds new layer risk. A senior wrapper therefore cannot automatically receive a better score than raw ONyc.

    Keep that conservative result until a documented, approved loss transformation supports different treatment. A mitigation record alone does not override the inheritance rule.

    Protection and access are different. Exponent describes senior withdrawal and yield pauses during recovery. Model a PT maturity falling inside that pause. Recovery mechanics.

    05 / What should happen first?

    Build the evidence from the foundation upward

    1 · Resolve identities

    Confirm the three token mints, PT markets and maturities, holding size, target cash asset, and plain versus leveraged exposure.

    2 · Inspect ONyc

    Read current participation rights, backing holdings, underwriting exposures, claims/reserves, attestations, controls, and available exits.

    3 · Add transformations

    Verify Exponent settlement, then srONyc’s waterfall and recovery states. Compare normal, early-exit, and stress routes before scoring.
